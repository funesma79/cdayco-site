Professional experience · 2024

Carolina Abril · Design Week Málaga

Carolina Abril was officially presented as Director of Design Week Málaga 2024. The Málaga edition brought together architecture, interior design, construction and design, with institutional support from Málaga City Council.

05.02.2024
Official presentation · Málaga City Council
Design Week Málaga 2024 was officially presented at the Patio de Banderas of Málaga City Hall. Institutional documentation explicitly identifies Carolina Abril as Director of Design Week Málaga.
09–15.09
Design Week Málaga · 2024 edition
Design Week Málaga 2024 took place from 9 to 15 September, with the Palacio de Ferias y Congresos de Málaga — FYCMA — as a key venue for professionals and companies connected with architecture, construction, interiors and design.
Direction

From Marbella to Málaga: a new stage for a professional design event.

Design Week Málaga followed the previous Design Week Marbella editions, bringing to Málaga a professional event focused on architecture, construction, interior design, design and interior architecture.

The 2024 institutional presentation identified Carolina Abril as Director of the event and highlighted its objective of connecting talent, industry and national and international professionals from the sector.

Announced initiatives included DWM Urban, with activities across the city; DWM Learn, focused on sharing knowledge with students; and a Free Construction Zone conceived for participating firms and studios.

This page documents Carolina Abril's professional experience through institutional and public sources. It does not attribute the organisation of Design Week Málaga to CDA & Co.
