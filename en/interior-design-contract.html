Approach

Identity, function and experience in one space.

Every interior project begins with how the space will actually be used, who will use it and what the environment needs to communicate.

From there, the design develops through spatial planning, materials, furniture, lighting and finishes, keeping the different decisions connected to one overall concept.

CDA & Co. works from Madrid on interior design and contract projects in Spain and is open to international collaborations where design and implementation can be coordinated effectively.

Process

From concept to installation.

Phase 01

Concept & Spatial Planning

We define the identity, use, spatial organisation and experience the project needs to support.

Phase 02

Design & Selection

Materials, furniture, lighting and finishes are developed as part of one coherent interior language.

Phase 03

Coordination & Installation

Suppliers and implementation are coordinated through to the final installation of the designed space.

Connected disciplines

Interior design connected with exhibitions, brands and events.

CDA & Co. approaches interior design as part of a wider spatial practice that also includes exhibition environments, brand spaces and event-related design.

This allows interior and contract projects to be considered not only as visual compositions, but as environments shaped by identity, movement, material choices and real use.
