Integrated Direction

From the event concept to what happens on site.

Event production is not only about organising individual tasks. It requires understanding how visitors arrive, what they encounter, how they move through the event and where meaningful interactions take place.

CDA & Co. approaches trade shows, exhibitions and professional events as connected systems: concept, spatial planning, exhibitors, suppliers, technical production, logistics, installation and operational coordination all need to support the same idea.

The studio works from Madrid on projects in Spain and is open to international collaborations where design, direction and production can be coordinated effectively.
