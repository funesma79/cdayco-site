Professional experience · 2019–2020

Carolina Abril · Marbella Design

Carolina Abril served as Executive Director of Marbella Design in 2019 and 2020. Her role is documented by independent publications covering interior design, design and architecture.

2019
Marbella Design · Second edition
Carolina Abril led the executive direction of the second edition of Marbella Design, held from 4 to 15 July 2019 at the Palacio de Ferias, Congresos y Exposiciones de Marbella.
2020
Marbella Design · Third edition
In 2020, Carolina Abril was again publicly identified as Executive Director of Marbella Design. The third edition took place from 24 September to 3 October, with interior design and the contract sector playing a prominent role.
Direction

Design, interiors and the direction of large-scale events.

Marbella Design brought together professionals, studios and brands from interior design, architecture, design and decoration in a large-scale exhibition format in Marbella.

Carolina Abril's executive direction of the 2019 and 2020 editions forms part of her documented professional experience prior to the current stage of CDA & Co.

This page documents that professional experience using independent public sources. It does not attribute the organisation of Marbella Design to CDA & Co.
