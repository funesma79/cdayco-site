Exhibiting in Spain

One team from the idea to the installation.

Exhibiting abroad means working with a new venue, a new calendar and a new set of exhibitor rules. We start from the official exhibitor manual of the specific fair to adjust the design to the conditions of the venue, and we define with each client the scope of the project: design only, or design with project direction, production and installation.

Stand design starts by understanding what has to happen inside the space: who visits it, what they need to discover and how they should move. From there we work on circulation, lighting, materials, signage and brand identity as one idea, with build-up and dismantling planned from the concept stage.

CDA & Co. is based in Madrid. You can read more about the studio or our event design and production work.

Process

From concept to installation.

Phase 01

Concept and brief

We define the purpose, the message and the experience the stand has to communicate, together with the floor area, budget and fair calendar.

Phase 02

Spatial design

We develop the layout, circulation, materials, lighting and graphic integration.

Phase 03

Production and installation

In general, projects are carried out with specialist suppliers and contractors that we coordinate. Each proposal defines how production, build-up and installation will be organised.

Getting started

What we need to start.

  • The fair, its dates and the venue.
  • The stand area and its position in the hall, if already assigned.
  • The goal of the stand: what you want to present, and to whom.
  • An indicative budget and the project timeline.
  • Your brand identity and the materials available.
  • The official exhibitor manual of the fair, if you already have it.

If you do not have all of this yet, write to us with what you have and we will shape it together.

Frequently asked questions

Frequently asked questions about stand design in Spain.

How much does stand design cost?

It depends on the floor area, the fair, the materials and the scope: design only, or design with production and installation. We work from a proposal. Tell us the fair, dates, stand size and scope, and we will outline an estimate.

How far in advance should we start?

As early as possible, because design, production and venue deadlines have to fit together. Each fair sets its own deadlines in its official exhibitor manual, which is the first thing we check.

Do you design and build the stand?

We work on the design, project direction, production and installation of the project. In general, projects are carried out with specialist suppliers and contractors that we coordinate. Each proposal defines how production, build-up and installation will be organised.

Which fairs can you work on?

We can plan a project for fairs across Spain, including venues such as IFEMA Madrid and Fira de Barcelona.
