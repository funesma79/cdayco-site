Studio

A multidisciplinary design studio.

CDA & Co. is not a communications agency or a consultancy. We design, direct and produce projects for brands and companies: trade fairs and large-scale events, stands and brand spaces, and Contract interior design.

We coordinate every project from start to finish — from concept to installation — together with suppliers, contractors and technical teams, so that what is designed gets built exactly as intended.

What we do.

  • —Fairs & Events — direction, coordination and production of trade fairs, exhibitions and large-scale events
  • —Stands & Brand Spaces — design and development of stands, pavilions and exhibition spaces
  • —Contract Interior Design — hospitality, retail, showrooms, offices and Contract projects
  • —International brand market entry in Spain and Europe
Team

CDA & Co. is made up of a multidisciplinary team, with direct experience designing, directing and producing trade fairs, events, temporary architecture and Contract interior design.
