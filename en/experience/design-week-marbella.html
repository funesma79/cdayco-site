Professional experience · 2021–2022

Carolina Abril · Design Week Marbella

Carolina Abril held leadership roles at Design Week Marbella in its 2021 and 2022 editions. Public sources document her executive and general direction responsibilities within the event dedicated to architecture, interiors, art and design.

2021
Design Week Marbella · 2021 edition
CIT Marbella identified Carolina Abril as Executive Director of Design Week Marbella. Another publication from the same year described her as General Director of the project. The edition took place from 27 September to 3 October 2021 in Marbella.
2022
Design Week Marbella · 2022 edition
In 2022, Carolina Abril was publicly identified as General Director of Design Week Marbella. The event took place from 22 to 28 September and brought together initiatives related to architecture, interior design, art and design.
Direction

Architecture, interiors and design in one professional event.

Design Week Marbella was conceived as a professional event around architecture, construction, design, interior design and interior architecture.

Carolina Abril's leadership roles in the 2021 and 2022 editions continued a documented professional trajectory linked to the direction and management of large-scale design events.

This page documents that professional experience through independent public sources. It does not attribute the organisation of Design Week Marbella to CDA & Co.
