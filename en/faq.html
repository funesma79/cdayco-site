Frequently asked questions

What clients usually ask us.

Practical answers about exhibition design, event design and production, interior design & contract, project scope, timing and how to work with CDA & Co.

What exactly does CDA & Co. do?

CDA & Co. is a multidisciplinary studio working across design, direction and production of spaces, exhibitions and large-scale events. Our work connects exhibition design, event design and production and interior design & contract, from concept through to final installation.

What is exhibition design?

We develop stands, pavilions, installations, scenography, showrooms and temporary environments for brands, institutions and events. The work combines spatial communication, visitor journey, materiality, lighting and execution. Explore exhibition design.

What is the difference between exhibition design and interior design & contract?

Exhibition design focuses primarily on temporary environments such as trade shows, events, stands and installations. Interior design & contract focuses on permanent professional, commercial and hospitality spaces, with materials, furniture, functionality and finishes developed for long-term use.

Does CDA & Co. also manage production, not only design?

Yes. We can coordinate a project from concept and design through suppliers, fabrication, logistics, installation and final delivery. We can manage a complete project or join at a specific phase, depending on the scope. Explore event design & production.

Does CDA & Co. work only in Madrid?

The studio is based in Madrid and works on projects in Spain. International collaboration opportunities can be assessed according to the location, scope and requirements of each project.

How long does it take to design and build a trade show stand or exhibition?

Timing depends on scale, technical requirements and project type. A stand, exhibition, permanent interior or large event can require very different schedules. Send us the event date, location, approximate size and initial scope and we can assess a realistic timeline.

What documented experience does Carolina Abril bring to trade shows and major design events?

Carolina Abril's documented professional experience includes the executive direction of Marbella Design in 2019 and 2020, direction responsibilities at Design Week Marbella in 2021 and 2022, and the direction of Design Week Málaga in 2024. You can review the professional profile and the external media and institutional sources.

How do I start a project with CDA & Co.?

Email hola@cdayco.com or use our contact form with the project type, location, approximate timing and initial scope. From there we arrange a conversation to understand the project in detail.
